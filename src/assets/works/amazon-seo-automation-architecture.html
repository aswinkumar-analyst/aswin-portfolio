02 · Execution Flow

One listing, end to end

Each listing goes through the same six steps. The loop then picks up the next product until the catalogue is done.

STEP 01

Queue the listing

The orchestrator takes the next product from the catalogue and starts the workflow for it.

STEP 02

Access the listing

Playwright opens the product's listing in Seller Central and reads its current title, description, and bullet points.

STEP 03

Send to Claude

The existing content goes to Claude as the starting point for optimization.

STEP 04

Generate SEO content

Claude returns rewritten copy that is more informative, consistent, and search-friendly for all three fields.

STEP 05

Publish the update

Playwright fills in the optimized content in the listing editor and submits the change.

STEP 06

Move to the next

The loop continues through the catalogue, applying the same standard to 700+ listings.
