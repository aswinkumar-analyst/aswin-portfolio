Overview

AI-generated listing content, published automatically

I designed and built an end-to-end automation workflow that optimizes Amazon product listings at catalogue scale. Claude rewrites each product's title, description, and key-feature bullet points into clearer, SEO-focused copy. Playwright then publishes the changes directly in Amazon Seller Central.

The result: more than 700 listings with more informative, consistent, search-friendly content, and none of them edited by hand.

The Challenge

Manual content editing couldn't keep up

Product content drives discoverability on Amazon, but keeping it up to date doesn't scale.

Each listing needed separate updates to three content areas. Across a catalogue of more than 700 products, that meant thousands of manual edits. The work was slow and repetitive, and quality and tone tended to vary from one listing to the next.

The core problem: content quality depended on manual effort, so it couldn't keep up as the catalogue grew.

The Solution

An AI-assisted content pipeline

I built a workflow that combines language-model content generation with browser automation. It handles the whole loop, from reading a listing to publishing the improved version.

01
Playwright

Access Listing

Opens each product listing in Seller Central and retrieves its current title, description, and bullets.

02
Claude

Enhance Content

Generates SEO-focused versions that are clearer, more informative, and keyword-aware, with consistent tone.

03
Playwright

Automate Update

Navigates the Seller Central UI, fills in the optimized content, and submits the listing update.

04
Pipeline

Scale Across Catalogue

Repeats the process for every product, so every listing gets the same standard of optimization.

Claude handles judgment

Writing good product copy takes language understanding and context. That's where the language model does the work.

Playwright handles repetition

Operating the Seller Central interface is predictable and repetitive, which makes it a good fit for reliable browser automation.
